About Me

I'm a Data Analyst who enjoys asking questions.

My approach to analysis revolves around identifying common or recurring problems, digging into the data, and figuring out why they are happening. I'm less interested in simply reporting what happened and more interested in understanding the story behind it — what changed, what might be causing it, and where the problem actually lies.

My toolkit includes Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, and an unhealthy amount of curiosity. I enjoy the entire process of working with data — from cleaning the wonderfully chaotic stuff that people insist is "ready to analyse," to building dashboards and reports that make the final answer easier to understand.

I'm constantly learning, experimenting, and finding new ways to approach problems — while of course, occasionally discovering that the reason my analysis wasn't working was because I forgot one comma three hours ago.

Data can tell you what happened. I want to know why.
